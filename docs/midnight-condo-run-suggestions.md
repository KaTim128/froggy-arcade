# Midnight Condo Run — improvement suggestions

These are suggestions only. Nothing in the game has been changed for this part,
apart from the price changes already agreed in M9: furniture ×1.5,
clothing ×1.2 and haircuts ×1.3.

The run as it plays now:

1. Nightfall outside the arcade, with $300 in your pocket and three lines of
   thought.
2. The walk east to Lily's Condos.
3. Checking in at the desk and paying the $300 in cash.
4. The lift up to Level 6, then room 612.
5. The night in the room: the window, the bed, the bathroom, the scare.

## Pacing and tension

1. **A sense of being followed on the walk there.** Add a few distant sounds
   behind you: a bin lid, a croak, footsteps that stop when you stop. Add one
   glance-back moment where the street behind you is empty. This sets up the
   night without showing Froggy too early.
2. **A tighter, darker approach.** Dim the street lamps one by one as you near
   the condos. The building's sign could buzz and flicker, with one letter out.
3. **A short "last chance" beat at the desk.** The receptionist could hesitate:
   *"Level 6? ...We usually put people on 2."* This hints that something is
   wrong with the floor before you see it.
4. **Two rhythms in the lift.** Have it pause for a moment between floors 4
   and 5, with the light dipping, then carry on. It costs nothing and makes
   the ride memorable. The freeze fix means a pause like this is now safe to
   add.

## The room

5. **Small, readable "something is off" details** that change each time you
   look:
   - the curtain tie undone;
   - the bathroom door ajar when you left it shut;
   - a wet footprint on the oak floor.
6. **A use for what you bought.** Furniture placed in the condo could matter at
   night. A lamp left on keeps one corner lit, and a wardrobe gives you
   somewhere to hide. This gives the higher M9 prices a payoff.
7. **A clock on the wall that moves on in real time** through the night, so
   the player knows how long they must last.

## Choice and replay

8. **Two ways through the night.** Either stay in and hide, or slip out by the
   stairs to the 2D stairwell. Each could lead to a different morning
   (a different badge or line).
9. **A slightly different run each night.** Change which sound comes first,
   which detail changes, and where the scare comes from (window, door or
   bathroom), so a second play is not a repeat.
10. **A wrong answer that doesn't punish.** If you open the window when you
    shouldn't, give a near-miss and a warning the first time, not an instant
    death. Save the instant death for a second mistake.

## Feedback and clarity

11. **One-line goals in the HUD**, matching the stealth phase. For example:
    *"CHECK IN — $300 CASH"*, then *"ROOM 612, LEVEL 6"*, then *"GET THROUGH
    THE NIGHT"*.
12. **Show the price before the walk.** The midnight thoughts could mention
    the $300 outright, so a player short of cash knows to earn more first
    instead of finding out at the desk.
13. **Phones:**
    - make the INTERACT prompt for the desk, lift and doors large;
    - keep the night scare's controls in the black band below the game, as
      the other horror scenes do.

## Audio

14. **A building sound bed** for the condos, unlike the arcade's: pipes
    ticking, a fridge hum, a neighbour's muffled TV that cuts out suddenly.
15. **Silence as a cue.** For the second before the scare, drop every
    ambient sound to nothing.
